Reservoir operations under uncertainty

Decision-support research for a multi-objective water system where today’s action changes tomorrow’s feasible choices.

Industrial researchForecasting · simulation · optimizationSelected details omitted

Context

Reservoir operations require sequential decisions under uncertain future conditions. Releases made now affect the future state of the system, while operational objectives and constraints may compete with one another.

Decision challenge

The difficulty is not only finding a mathematical optimum. Operational rules, uncertain inputs, multiple performance criteria, and domain interpretation all affect whether a policy is useful.

My contribution

My work focused on translating operational requirements into a mathematical and computational framework, developing evaluation and optimization methodology, and connecting model outputs with domain interpretation.

Approach

  • Formulate operational objectives and constraints at a decision-relevant level.
  • Evaluate alternatives under multiple future scenarios.
  • Use forecasting and simulation as inputs to decision analysis.
  • Optimize candidate decisions or operating policies and compare them using operational performance criteria.

Outcome

The framework supports systematic comparison of operational alternatives across scenarios and objectives. Detailed client information, parameters, architecture, algorithms, and quantitative results are omitted.

In applied OR, the hardest part is often deciding what the optimization problem should be—not solving the final mathematical program.

My role

  • Problem formulation
  • Computational methodology
  • Scenario-based evaluation
  • Translation of domain requirements

Methods

  • Time-series forecasting
  • Simulation
  • Optimization
  • Multi-objective analysis

Confidentiality

Client identity, system topology, operational parameters, proprietary implementation, and non-public performance metrics are not disclosed.
