Teaching Approach

I prefer to begin with a system that students can reason about intuitively, such as waiting lines, inventory decisions, allocation problems, or sequential decisions under uncertainty. From there, I introduce the mathematical abstraction, work through the analysis, and return to interpretation and implementation.

For students with different mathematical backgrounds, I use visual explanation, small hand-worked examples, and computational experiments before moving to more formal derivations. The goal is not only to apply a formula, but to understand what the model assumes, what its result means, and where it may fail.
